The challenge

Context gets lost between channels.

When customer history is split across tools, agents spend time searching for facts and teams spend time guessing who owns the next move. A queue can look full while the useful context remains somewhere else.

  • Repeated handoffsImportant details move between people without a shared case view.
  • Unclear contextAgents have to reconstruct the customer story before they can help.
  • Hard-to-see patternsTeams cannot easily see where questions repeat or where work waits.
The support model

One operating layer, clear handovers.

Bring conversations, routing, knowledge, and agent support into a shared workflow. People keep ownership; AI helps with the repeatable work around each case.

  • Keep customer context beside the conversation
  • Guide work with clear triage and escalation rules
  • Give agents useful suggestions without removing human judgment
  • Use operational insight to improve the next workflow
Support capabilities

Make the work easier to see, route, and improve.

Customer Support connects the moments that usually get separated: self-service, conversation handling, agent support, escalation, and learning.

Bring channel conversations, customer history, and case notes into one place agents can work from.

Triage and routing

Common questions

Clear answers before you build.

A support workspace should make responsibility clearer, not make the people responsible harder to see.

Is AI meant to replace support agents?
No. AI assists with summaries, suggestions, and drafts. People remain responsible for understanding the case, making decisions, and communicating with the customer.
Can we start with the channels we already use?
The right starting point follows your real channel mix, knowledge sources, and team boundaries. Exact channel coverage and integrations should be confirmed during discovery so the workflow matches how your people already work.
How does human escalation work?
Escalation rules can respond to urgency, sensitivity, uncertainty, or a customer request. The person receiving the case gets the conversation context and the next action, rather than starting from a blank page.
Will AI send customer replies on its own?
Use AI for suggestions and preparation, not blind sending. Teams decide what to edit, approve, and send, with human review kept in place for consequential replies.
